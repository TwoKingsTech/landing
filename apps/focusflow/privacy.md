## What data we collect

**None.** FocusFlow does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All session history and settings are stored exclusively on your device using local storage. No analytics SDK, crash reporter, or advertising framework is included in the app.

## Device permissions

FocusFlow requests no special device permissions beyond what is required to display notifications for timer alerts. Notification permission is entirely optional — the app functions fully without it.

## Third-party services

FocusFlow does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

## Children's privacy

FocusFlow is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because we collect no data at all, there is no risk of inadvertently collecting children's information.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that change our data practices, we will update this page and bump the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
