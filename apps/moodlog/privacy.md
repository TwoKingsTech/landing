## Who we are

MoodLog is developed and maintained by **Code Trade Kft**, a limited company registered in Hungary. We operate under the brand name **Two Kings Tech**.

Contact: [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }})

## What data we collect

**None.** MoodLog does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Your mood entries and notes are stored only on your device. We have no server, no account system, and no way to access your data — because we never receive it.

## Device permissions

MoodLog may request the following optional permissions:

- **Notifications** — for daily reminder prompts (can be disabled in system settings at any time)
- **FaceID / TouchID** — only if you enable the passcode lock feature; biometric data never leaves your device

## Third-party services

MoodLog does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

## Data export and deletion

All your data lives on your device. To delete it, simply uninstall the app. There is no account to delete or server-side data to request removal of.

## Children's privacy

MoodLog is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no data, there is no risk of inadvertently processing children's information.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that change our data practices, we will update this page and bump the "Last updated" date above.
