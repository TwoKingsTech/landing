## Data we store on your device

Stoic Quotes stores the following data **locally on your device only**, using the operating system's sandboxed local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any server we operate.

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Your first name | Personalising the in-app experience |
| Selected themes / categories | Filtering quotes to your interests |
| Favourite quotes | Saving quotes you want to revisit |
| Per-quote reminder times | Scheduling your chosen quote reminders |
| Notification preferences | Remembering whether reminders are enabled and your default time |
| Colour theme preference | Remembering your appearance setting |
| Seen quote IDs | Avoiding showing the same quote twice in a row |
| Daily quote cache | Storing today's quote for offline access |

**Deleting the app removes all of this data permanently.** There is no account to delete and no server-side copy to request.

## Third-party services

While we do not collect or transmit your data ourselves, Stoic Quotes integrates two third-party SDKs that may collect data independently under their own privacy policies.

### Google AdMob (advertising)

Stoic Quotes displays ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect:

- **Advertising identifier** — IDFA on iOS (subject to App Tracking Transparency consent) or Google Advertising ID on Android
- **IP address** — used to infer approximate location for ad targeting
- **Device information** — model, operating system version, screen size
- **Ad interaction data** — impressions, clicks, and view duration

On iOS, AdMob will only access your advertising identifier after you explicitly grant permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. You may withdraw this permission at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

On any platform, you can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings:
- iOS: **Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Personalised Ads** (off)
- Android: **Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID**

Google's privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### Sentry (crash reporting)

Stoic Quotes uses **Sentry** to automatically report application crashes so we can identify and fix bugs. When a crash occurs, Sentry may transmit:

- Device model and operating system version
- App version
- Stack trace (code location of the crash)
- Application state at the time of the crash

We do not intentionally send any personally identifiable information to Sentry. Sentry's privacy policy: [https://sentry.io/privacy/](https://sentry.io/privacy/)

## Push notifications

All notifications in Stoic Quotes are **local notifications** — they are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device with no server involvement. We do not use push notification services and no notification-related data is transmitted anywhere.

You can disable notifications at any time in **Settings → Notifications → Stoic Quotes**.

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR (EU/EEA), UK GDPR, CCPA (California), or other applicable laws, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data.
